# Progression & Economy

## Goal

Create regular, visible progress while protecting PvP fairness and build diversity.

## Progression layers

### Account level / XP

Represents broad player progression.

Can unlock:

- new hull classes;
- additional equipment families;
- visual rewards;
- feature access;
- profile prestige.

Account level should not directly create unlimited combat stat scaling.

### Equipment upgrades

Players improve owned components using credits/materials.

Upgrade design principles:

- shallow enough that matchmaking can remain fair;
- visible improvement;
- avoid exponential stat curves;
- later levels can favor specialization/sidegrades instead of raw numbers.

### Ship Power

A normalized summary used for:

- player-facing comparison;
- early matchmaking guardrails;
- detecting extreme loadout mismatch.

Power is **not** a substitute for ranked skill rating.

## Currencies

### Credits

Earned from:

- battles;
- wins/losses with different reward weights;
- missions later;
- achievements later.

Spent on:

- equipment upgrades;
- unlocks;
- basic progression costs.

### Upgrade materials

Used to add texture to progression without creating too many currencies.

MVP should keep this minimal—ideally one or two material classes.

### Premium currency — later

Only if monetization is introduced. Prefer cosmetics and convenience that does not influence ranked combat power.

## Battle rewards

Every completed legitimate battle should give some progress. Losing must not feel like zero-value time.

Reward structure:

- base participation XP;
- credits;
- victory bonus;
- first-win or streak systems later;
- ranked rating handled separately.

## Crates / random rewards

If crates are used later:

- avoid making paid random power acquisition the core economy;
- disclose odds where required;
- prefer cosmetic/random-material utility over competitive exclusives.

MVP does not require crates.

## Leagues

Initial ladder:

1. Bronze
2. Silver
3. Gold
4. Platinum
5. Diamond
6. Master

Each league can have subdivisions after population supports it.

## Seasons

Post-MVP seasons can provide:

- partial rating reset;
- cosmetic rewards;
- seasonal badge;
- leaderboard history;
- themed visual content.

Do not reset owned equipment each season.

## Build archetypes

Progression must preserve multiple viable identities:

### Tank

- high hull / armor;
- sustain;
- low burst.

### Glass Cannon

- high burst;
- low durability;
- wins through timing.

### Shield Specialist

- high shield value / recovery;
- vulnerable to specific counters.

### Disruptor

- energy/system pressure;
- lower raw damage;
- tempo control.

Additional archetypes can emerge from equipment rather than explicit classes.

## Monetization guardrail

The preferred rule:

> Money may change how a ship looks much more than how strong it is in ranked PvP.

Strong monetization candidates:

- ship skins;
- paint/decals;
- weapon effects;
- engine trails;
- kill/victory effects;
- avatars;
- banners;
- emotes;
- seasonal cosmetics.

## Economy telemetry

Track:

- credits earned/spent per day;
- upgrade time-to-next-meaningful-choice;
- inventory usage;
- item pick rate;
- win rate by equipment level and archetype;
- power-rating correlation;
- churn after losses;
- percentage of players who adjust loadout after a match.
